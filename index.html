Our History

1886

The Original Formula

Dr. John Pemberton created Coca-Cola at his pharmacy in Atlanta, Georgia. The iconic beverage was first served at Jacobs' Pharmacy for five cents a glass.

1915

The Contour Bottle

The iconic contour bottle was introduced, designed to be recognizable even in the dark. This revolutionary design became one of the most recognized packages in the world.
